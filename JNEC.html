About College

Jawaharlal Nehru Engineering College (JNEC) is the flagship engineering college of MGM University, located in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra. Established in 1983, the institute offers undergraduate engineering programs in computer science, artificial intelligence, civil, mechanical, electrical, chemical and electronics-related disciplines.
