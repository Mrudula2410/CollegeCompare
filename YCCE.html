About College

Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering (YCCE) is an autonomous engineering institute located at Wanadongri, Nagpur, Maharashtra. The institute offers undergraduate engineering programs across computer science, electronics, electrical, mechanical and civil engineering disciplines.
