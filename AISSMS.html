About College

All India Shri Shivaji Memorial Society's College of Engineering (AISSMS COE) is an engineering institute located in Pune, Maharashtra. The college offers undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs and provides students with opportunities for technical learning, projects, practical training and industry interaction.
