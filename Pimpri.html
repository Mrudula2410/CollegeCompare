About College

Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE) is an autonomous engineering institute located in Nigdi, Pune. The institute is run by Pimpri Chinchwad Education Trust and is affiliated with Savitribai Phule Pune University. PCCOE offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs with a focus on technical education, practical learning, research and industry-oriented skills.
