About College

Ramdeobaba University, formerly known as Shri Ramdeobaba College of Engineering and Management (RCOEM), is an engineering and technology institute located in Nagpur, Maharashtra. The institute offers undergraduate programs across computer science, electronics, electrical, civil, mechanical, industrial and biomedical engineering.
